Use case: logo-brand. Single square master app-icon artwork for BeeCount, a friendly personal ledger. Make a memorable, slightly deadpan editorial mascot: a little bumblebee has pulled a big OPEN pocket ledger up under her nose and is absorbed in the accounts. Extreme close-up, straight-on graphic portrait. The head is a single broad warm marigold shape with dark side patches, two short bent antennae with soft tips, two small oval ink eyes glancing DOWN at the pages. Two simple pale wing lobes show slightly behind the shoulders so it reads unmistakably as a bee. A sliver of striped belly if natural. Tiny mitten-like dark paws hold the book at the upper outer edges. Express quiet concentration with the eyes and posture alone.
Make the BOOK itself distinctive: a squat, strong deep petrol-blue open hardback at the bottom, a clear shallow V at the spine; two thick cream page edges describe the top opening. A little persimmon-red ribbon hangs from the lower edge. No marks on the cover, no lines of tiny text. Book and bee form ONE compact, pleasing outer silhouette. Enlarge face, crop out all legs; a human should be able to sketch this from memory.
Art direction: adult, witty independent bookshop imprint. Broad hand-cut curves, optical asymmetry, confident overlapping planes of perfectly flat spot ink. No uniform cartoon outlines; the dark blue is used as contour in just a few essential places. Warm pale oat square field edge-to-edge, vivid saffron, very dark petrol, warm milk white, one tiny vermilion accent. Keep the image flat and clean like a contemporary linocut simplified into flawless vector shapes. Subject fills 76% of square with generous margins on all sides. Her eyes, wings, and the V of the ledger must read at 32px. Original character, charming through an observed action. No cheeks, no smile, no eyelashes, no fur, no kawaii emoji, no 3D, no gradients, no gloss, no borders, no words, no letterforms, no type, no surrounding mockup. One integrated logo, no sheet of variations.
